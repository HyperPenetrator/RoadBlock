Privacy Policy

1. Philosophy & Commitment

At RoadFireWall, we understand that tactical navigation requires immense trust. Your route and location are your private business. We engineer our platform to process telemetry locally on-device as much as theoretically possible.

2. Location Data & Telemetry

To provide high-performance grid navigation, our app requires precise geolocation permissions. This data is rigorously handled:

  • Batched & Encrypted: Any data leaving the device is securely batched and encrypted in transit.
  • Zero Selling: Location data is strictly used for functional state management. It is never sold to third parties or used for ad targeting.
  • Local Preference: Most telemetry filters (like our Kalman approach) are executed strictly on your device's hardware.

3. GDPR Compliance

As a user, you retain full rights over your personal data. You may request access, rectification, or complete erasure of any server-side logs associated with your telemetry at any time. Address all grid compliance inquiries to our support node.
